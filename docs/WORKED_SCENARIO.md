# Worked Scenario — Suez Canal blockage

**Illustrative assumptions only.** Starting trade-finance portfolio: USD 500 million. Scenario: severity 5, duration 12 days, freight cost shock +65%, shipment delay 10 days, inventory buffer +8 days, LC expiry risk 18%, insurance claim factor 12%.

## Shock channels
1. Freight cost increase: additional logistics cost affects borrower margins and facility utilisation.
2. Shipment delay: goods arrive later, receivables are collected later, and CCC can extend.
3. Inventory buffer: additional inventory ties up working capital.
4. LC expiry risk: delayed shipment/document presentation can create amendment, expiry or settlement exposure.
5. Cargo insurance: damaged/lost cargo can trigger claims, subject to policy wording and exclusions.

## Decision choices
- Extend/amend LC: reduce the scenario's modelled LC expiry component at an immediate liquidity cost.
- Diversify routes/carriers: reduce freight and delay exposure at an immediate implementation cost.
- Arrange working capital: reduce inventory-stress impact and improve liquidity, with financing cost.
- Reprice: increase yield but may increase borrower stress.
- Monitor: use intelligence signals and reduce risk score in the simplified game.

The app displays the modelled impacts separately. These mechanics are designed for learning and are not a bank's actual loss methodology.
